Manufacturing Process

From Clean Water to Solid Ice

Five controlled steps that keep every block consistent and hygienic.

1

Water Filtration

Water is filtered and treated before it enters the moulds.

2

Freezing

Moulds are frozen in chilled brine tanks for dense, uniform blocks.

3

Extraction

Frozen blocks are carefully released from the moulds.

4

Quality Check

Each batch is inspected for clarity, size and weight.

5

Storage & Dispatch

Blocks move to cold storage, then onto delivery vehicles.
